# Surface brief · Landing (/) and ticket activation (/e/[code])

Scope: public pages. Landing is Persuade; activation is Operate inside the same world.
Audience: parishioners holding a paper ticket; job: activate it, pay, pick a table; or find out how to get one.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The paper ticket is the protagonist. The page is built from the ticket and the boulevard map, not from a hero photo over a row of feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Cream ticket-stock ground, whole fields of Christmas red and pine green, gold only as ornament or on dark fields, warm brown-black ink. Young Serif display (70s gaita LP covers), Red Hat Text for everything else (open, legible at small sizes on phones at night). Perforated stub edges with semicircle notches; string lights over the boulevard map.

STORY: Your ticket is paper; scan its QR, report your Zelle/Pago Móvil payment, choose your table while there is room. Tables fill live.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Red field carrying the event name huge in cream, date and place beneath; the real ticket object crossing the red/cream boundary, its QR working (opens /activar); a code field with "Activar" as the working action; a live line "N mesas con cupo · M llenas".

FORM: Ticket + map world, pinned by the user (Navidad zuliana cálida, clara). seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
